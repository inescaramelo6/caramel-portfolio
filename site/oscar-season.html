01 —

the brief

NOS Cinemas — the leading cinema chain in Portugal — needed a face for their Oscar season campaign. One illustrated character who could front the whole run, from cinema posters and outdoor advertising to a daily Facebook content series. He had to be flexible enough to live across formats, but recognisable in a single glance.

The brief was as much about building a community as moving tickets: could an illustrated character become someone people actually wanted to follow on social, all the way to the ceremony?

02 —

what I did

Character design

Designed the lead character — silhouette, palette, expression set — and a system for posing him across awards-season scenarios.

OOH artwork

Illustrations adapted for buses, billboards and cinemas — same character, different formats and crops, all on-brand.

Daily social content

Facebook posts featuring the character reacting to nominations, big moments, and red-carpet looks — feeding a daily content drumbeat.

Community building

Tone of voice, comment hooks and weekly drops that turned viewers into followers, then followers into a 363K-strong community.
